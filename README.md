Download and install iLoader v2.4.1 for Windows.



Install iTunes on Windows.



Download either Aurora-widget.ipa or Aurora-no-widget.ipa from the Music release.



Connect your unlocked iPhone using USB.



Tap Trust This Computer on your iPhone.



Open iLoader and follow its Apple Account signing prompts.



Select your connected device.



Choose the IPA import/install option.



Browse to your downloaded Music IPA.



Follow the signing and installation prompts.



Trust the developer profile in iPhone Settings if requested.



Open Music from your Home Screen.
